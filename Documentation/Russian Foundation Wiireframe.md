# Russian Foundations
## Foundation Wireframe
### Version 0.1

---

## Purpose
The Russian Foundations Wireframe defines the learner experience for the Russian Foundations landing page. 
This document translates the Michael's Foundations Framework into the first complete Foundation implementation. 
The goal is to create an engaging, confidence-building learning experience that welcomes learners of every 
experience level while guiding them toward meaningful progress.

---

# Learner Experience Goal
A learner should understand the following within the first 30 seconds:

• What Russian Foundations is.
• Why it is different from other learning resources.
• Where they should begin.
• How their progress will be tracked.
• What success looks like.

If these five questions are answered, the learner should feel confident enough to begin learning.

---

# The 30-Second Rule
Within the first thirty seconds, the learner should think:

"Yes...

This course understands where I am.
- It gives me a clear place to begin.
- I can see where this journey will take me.
- I want to keep exploring."

---

# Section 1 — Foundation Hero

## Purpose
The Foundation Hero is the learner’s first introduction to Russian Foundations.

It should immediately:

- Explain what Russian Foundations is.
- Communicate the value of the learning experience.
- Help the learner imagine meaningful progress.
- Provide a clear next step.
- Invite learners who are uncertain to receive guidance.

---

## Proposed Hero Content

### Russian Foundations

#### Learn Russian with confidence.

Build practical reading, listening, speaking, vocabulary, and cultural understanding through a structured 
learning experience designed for beginners and returning learners. Russian Foundations will help you 
identify the path that fits your experience, track your progress, and build confidence one meaningful 
step at a time.

### Primary Actions

- Begin Learning
- Help Me Decide

---

## Learner Questions Answered
The Hero should answer:

1. What is Russian Foundations?
2. What will I learn?
3. Is this appropriate for my experience level?
4. How do I begin?
5. What should I do if I am unsure where to start?

---

## Desired Learner Reaction
The learner should think:

> “This feels clear, welcoming, and manageable. I understand what I can learn, and I know what to do next.”

---

# Section 2 — Choose Your Learning Path

## Purpose
This section helps learners begin at a point that matches their current experience rather than assuming 
every learner starts in the same place. The goal is to reduce uncertainty, build confidence, and create 
a personalized learning experience from the very beginning.

---

## Learning Paths

### 🌱 I'm Completely New to Russian
Recommended for learners with little or no previous experience.

Suggested Starting Point:
• Russian Alphabet

---

### 📖 I Know a Little Russian
Recommended for learners who recognize some vocabulary or have studied Russian before.

Suggested Starting Point:
• Essential Vocabulary

---

### 🎯 I'm Returning to Russian
Recommended for learners returning after months or years away from the language.

Suggested Starting Point:
• Grammar & Conversation Review

---

### 🧭 Help Me Decide
Take a short Learning Assessment. Russian Foundations will recommend a starting point 
based on your current experience. The recommendation is always optional.

Learners remain free to choose any learning path.

---

## Educational Philosophy
Traditional online courses often assume every learner should begin in the same place.
Michael's Foundations recognizes that every learner begins from a different starting point.
Rather than forcing a single path, learners receive thoughtful guidance while maintaining 
complete control over their learning journey.
