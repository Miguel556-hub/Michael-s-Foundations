Russian Foundations

Your first step is simpler than you think!

Russian is learned by using it. Read it. Hear it. Say it. Repeat it. Learning a new language can feel uncomfortable at first, and that's okay. Don't worry about being perfect. Every time you try, you understand a little more, remember a little more, and become more confident. But learning Russian is more than learning words. You'll learn how the language works and discover the people, culture, history, and places behind it. Take it one step at a time. That's how your journey begins.
