Lesson 1 · УРОК ПЕРВЫЙ

Numbers 0–10

Meet the core digits upon which all other numbers build.

Hear It – Say It

Select Hear beside a Russian number, then say it aloud. Listen for stress and the soft sign.

Number Cyrillic Pronunciation guide Listen
0 ноль nol’
1 один a-DEEN
2 два dva
3 три tree

5 пять pyat’
6 шесть shest’
7 семь syem’
8 восемь VO-syem’
9 девять DYE-vyat’
10 десять DYE-syat’

Uses your browser's Russian speech voice when available.

Under the Microscope

Grammar & Helpful Hints

  • Numbers 1–10 are unique words. They don't follow one simple pattern, so learn each one individually.
  • Stress matters. Most of these words have one stressed syllable; listen carefully to четыре and восемь .
  • Gender matters. один changes with nouns: одна (feminine), одно (neuter).
  • Soft endings. Notice the final ь in пять, шесть, семь, восемь, девять . The soft sign is not pronounced as a separate sound.
  • Combine easily. These forms become building blocks for larger numbers.

Lesson 2 · УРОК ВТОРОЙ

Numbers 11–19

Discover how Russian builds the teen numbers. Listen, notice the pattern, and construct numbers yourself.

Hear It – Say It

Select Hear beside each number, then repeat it aloud. Listen for the stressed syllable.

Number Cyrillic Pronunciation guide Listen
11 одиннадцать a-DEEN-na-tsat
12 двенадцать dvye-NA-tsat
13 тринадцать tree-NA-tsat
14 четырнадцать che-TYR-na-tsat
15 пятнадцать pyat-NA-tsat
16 шестнадцать shest-NA-tsat
17 семнадцать syem-NA-tsat
18 восемнадцать vo-syem-NA-tsat
19 девятнадцать dye-vyat-NA-tsat

Uses your browser's Russian speech voice when available.

Under the Microscope

Grammar & Helpful Hints

  • Look for the shared ending. Every number from 11–19 ends in -надцать.
  • The first piece can change. Compare пять with пятнадцать and семь with семнадцать.
  • Notice eleven. Одиннадцать has a doubled н at the join.
  • Listen for stress. In these numbers, the stressed syllable is not always in the same position.
  • Build the whole word. Join the first piece and надцать without a space.

Lesson 3 · УРОК ТРЕТИЙ

Numbers (10, 20, 30, ... 90)

Learn the teen pattern's next big cousin: the tens!

Hear It – Say It

Select Hear beside each Russian number. Repeat what you hear five to ten times.

Number Cyrillic Pronunciation guide Listen
10 десять DYE-syat'
20 двадцать DVA-tsat'
30 тридцать TREE-tsat'
40 сорок SO-rak
50 пятьдесят pyat'-dee-SYAT
60 шестьдесят shest'-dee-SYAT
70 семьдесят SYEM'-dee-syat
80 восемьдесят VO-syem'-dee-syat
90 девяносто dye-vee-NO-sta

Uses your browser's Russian speech voice when available.

Grammar & Helpful Hints

  • Look for a pattern. Thirty uses -дцать. Fifty through eighty use forms ending in -десят.
  • Meet the exceptions. Forty is сорок; ninety is девяносто.
  • Listen for stress. Fifty and sixty stress -ся́т; seventy and eighty stress the first part.
  • Remember twenty. Двадцать has its own form; it does not simply add -десят.

Lesson 4 · УРОК ЧЕТВЁРТЫЙ

Hundreds — 100, 200, 300 … 900

Meet the hundreds and discover how their endings change.

Hear It – Say It

Select Hear, listen carefully, and repeat each Russian number aloud.

Number Cyrillic Pronunciation guide Listen
100 сто sto
200 двести DVYE-stee
300 триста TREE-sta
400 четыреста che-TY-re-sta
500 пятьсот pyat-SOT
600 шестьсот shest-SOT
700 семьсот syem-SOT
800 восемьсот vo-syem-SOT
900 девятьсот dye-vyat-SOT

Uses your browser's Russian speech voice when available.

Grammar & Helpful Hints

  • One hundred stands alone: сто.
  • Two hundred: двести. Notice the special ending -сти.
  • Three and four hundred: триста and четыреста. Look for -ста.
  • Five through nine hundred: пятьсот, шестьсот, семьсот, восемьсот, девятьсот. They share -сот.
  • Listen for stress: пятьсо́т through девятьсо́т stress the final syllable.
